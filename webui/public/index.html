HOW IT WORKS

Built around real campus operations

KUET WasteX in four moving parts.

Bin telemetry

Every campus bin is modeled with a live fill level and fill rate; thresholds classify each bin from Normal to Critical across the day.

Priority engine

Bins are ranked by fill level and fill rate, so the most urgent bins are always collected first.

Shortest routes

The campus road network finds the shortest path between any two locations, so trucks never waste time on campus.

Live operations

A collection truck follows the priority order along real OpenStreetMap road geometry while drivers log each pickup from their portal.
